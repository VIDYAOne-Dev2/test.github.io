How to Record a Meeting Session

Please record all your training and discussion sessions so that you never miss any instructions or guidance. Steps for Windows 10 and Windows 11 below.

Before You Start Windows 10 Windows 11 After Recording Troubleshooting

Before You Start

Quick checklist to get a clean recording.

  • Inform participants that the meeting is being recorded and get their consent, as per company policy.
  • Join the meeting first and check that your microphone and speakers work.
  • Close private tabs, chats and notifications that you do not want captured.
  • Keep enough free disk space (about 100–200 MB per 10 minutes).

WINDOWS 10

Record a Meeting using Xbox Game Bar

Game Bar records the active app window (for example Teams, Zoom or your browser), not the whole desktop.

1

Turn on Xbox Game Bar (first time only)

Open Settings › Gaming › Xbox Game Bar and switch the toggle to On.

2

Join your meeting and click on its window

Make sure the meeting window is active (click anywhere inside it). Game Bar will not record File Explorer or the desktop.

3

Open Game Bar

Press Win + G together. The Game Bar overlay appears on screen.

4

Check your microphone, then start recording

In the Capture widget, click the microphone icon if you want your voice recorded, then click the round Start recording button. Shortcut: Win + Alt + R.

5

Continue your meeting

A small recording bar with a timer appears. You can click away and carry on with the meeting normally.

6

Stop recording

Click the Stop button on the recording bar, or press Win + Alt + R again. A “Game clip recorded” message appears.

7

Find your recording

Open This PC › Videos › Captures. The file is saved as an MP4.

WINDOWS 11

Record a Meeting using Snipping Tool

Snipping Tool records any area of your screen that you select.

1

Open Snipping Tool

Click Start, type Snipping Tool and press Enter.

2

Switch to Record mode and click New

Click the camcorder (Record) icon, then click New. Shortcut: Win + Shift + R.

3

Select the meeting window area

Drag your mouse to draw a box around the meeting window, then click Start. Recording begins after a 3-second countdown.

4

Stop recording

When the meeting ends, click the red Stop button on the recording bar.

5

Preview and save

Review the video in the preview window, then click Save (disk icon). Recordings usually go to Videos › Screen Recordings.

After Recording

Name, check and share the recording.

  • Rename the file clearly, e.g. Sprint-Review_18Nov2026.mp4.
  • Play it once to confirm that both video and audio were captured.
  • Share only via the approved company location (shared drive or Teams/SharePoint). Do not share on personal channels.
  • If the file is large, trim it or compress it before sharing.
Tip: Test a 30-second recording before an important meeting.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Win + G does nothingTurn on Xbox Game Bar in Settings › Gaming, then try again.
Game Bar says “Gaming features aren’t available”Click on the meeting window first so it is the active app (not the desktop or File Explorer).
No audio in the recordingCheck that the microphone is on in the recording tool and not muted in Windows or in the meeting app. Also check the audio options in Snipping Tool.
Cannot find the videoCheck Videos › Captures (Windows 10) or Videos › Screen Recordings (Windows 11).
Recording stopped suddenlyCheck free disk space and keep the laptop plugged in and awake during long meetings.
